# Setup questions

Ask these conversationally, two or three at a time. Skip anything the creator already answered.
Accept "I don't know yet" and leave that line marked "not set" in the profile rather than guessing.

## About you
1. Your name and your creator brand name.
2. The email address brands should use for collabs. Is it the same Gmail account you connected to
   Claude, or an alias / different inbox? Do you use a Gmail label for collab mail? What is it called?
3. Your main platforms (Amazon storefront, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, blog, Pinterest, other).
4. Are you an Amazon Influencer or Amazon Associate? (Turns on the Amazon rules.)
5. Your niches, and any categories you never take (for example supplements, alcohol, weapons).

## Your rates
6. What does a gifted product get the brand? (Example: one storefront video, nothing else.)
7. Your paid tiers and prices, if you have them (for example a dedicated YouTube review, a social
   bundle).
8. Your UGC rates: price per video, and what usage rights cost (term and channels).
9. Add-ons: rush fee, extra video, exclusivity, perpetual rights.
10. Your lowest acceptable number on each paid tier (kept private, never quoted).

## How you work
11. Do you only review products you have in hand?
12. How many gifted deals a week can you realistically take?
13. Your turnaround after a product arrives (in days).
14. Any extra non-negotiables (for example no scripted reviews, watermark until paid).
15. Proof points you are happy to share with brands (sales results, view counts, ratings). Only
    numbers you can stand behind.

## Your voice
16. Paste one or two emails you have sent to brands that sound like you.
17. Words or phrases you never want in your emails. Your usual sign-off.
